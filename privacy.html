Advertise to Yourself: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Advertise to Yourself does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

The goals you enter (a title, a start date and a due date) are stored with chrome.storage.sync, inside your own browser. If you have Chrome sync turned on, Chrome syncs them to your other Chrome browsers through your Google account, the same way it syncs your settings. The developer never receives them.

The extension makes no network requests, contains no analytics and loads no remote code.

Source code: github.com/rsktaker/advertise-to-yourself
